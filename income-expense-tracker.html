Why I Built This

I kept meeting the same problem, over and over, in different versions. Someone would download the free budget planner, fill it in by hand for a month, and feel real clarity for the first time. Then the next month would start, and they'd be back to guessing, because writing everything down by hand doesn't scale once life gets busy.

A budget tells you where you want your money to go. But once the month is underway, you need something that keeps up with what's actually happening, without you doing the math yourself every single time.

That's the gap this tracker was built to close.

It's not a fancier spreadsheet. It's a system: you log a transaction, and everything else, totals, categories, charts, the full picture, updates on its own.

Who This Is For

Personal finance, freelancers, and small business owners who want real clarity on their money without learning spreadsheet formulas or paying for accounting software.
